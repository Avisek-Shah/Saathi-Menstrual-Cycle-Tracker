# How this app predicts your period

It looks at the dates of your recent periods and weights the more recent ones more heavily. It takes the average of those cycles and projects forward to guess when your next one will start.

It also guesses when you might ovulate — about 14 days before the predicted period start. That 14-day assumption is the main reason the fertile window is a rough estimate. Your body doesn't always follow a clock.

So the predictions are helpful guides, not exact schedules. The more cycles you log, the better they get — but they will never be perfect.
